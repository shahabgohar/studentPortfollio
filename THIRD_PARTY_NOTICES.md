# Third-party notices

This site ships a small amount of third-party material. Their licences require
the notice to travel with the copies, so they are kept here and next to the
files themselves.

## Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1)

- **Archivo**: Copyright 2020 The Archivo Project Authors
  (https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo).
  Shipped as `public/fonts/archivo-var.v1.woff2`, a Latin subset of the variable
  font with the weight axis limited to 400-900. Full licence:
  `public/fonts/OFL-Archivo.txt`.
- **Martian Mono**: Copyright 2021 The Martian Mono Project Authors
  (https://github.com/evilmartians/mono).
  Shipped as `public/fonts/martian-mono-var.v1.woff2`, a Latin subset with the
  weight axis limited to 400-700. Full licence: `public/fonts/OFL-MartianMono.txt`.

Neither font declares a Reserved Font Name, so the subsets keep the original
family names. If a font file is re-subset, keep its OFL text beside it.
